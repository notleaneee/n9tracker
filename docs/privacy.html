Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

N9 Tracker is a free Discord bot. This policy explains what data it stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we store

Only when you link your Riot account with /valorant add:

DataWhy
Your Discord user IDTo know which Discord user the linked account belongs to
The Discord server ID where you linkedTo keep each server's leaderboard separate
Your Riot ID (Name#TAG) and PUUIDTo identify your Riot account when requesting your match data
The date you linkedRecord keeping
Stats from your matchesTo show your recent matches and the server leaderboard

What we don't store

  • Your Riot password. You sign in on Riot's own website through Riot Sign-On, and N9 Tracker never sees your credentials.
  • Your Discord messages.
  • Data about anyone who hasn't linked their own account.

Where your data is shown

Your linked account and stats are shown only in the Discord server where you linked them. Linking in one server doesn't share your data with any other server.

Where your data comes from

Match data comes only from the official Riot Games API, for accounts whose owner signed in with Riot. We don't use scraping or unofficial data sources.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data with anyone. There are no ads or trackers.

Deleting your data

Run /valorant remove in a server to unlink your account and immediately delete your data from that server. If the bot is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/notleaneee/n9tracker/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too.
