The service

N9 Tracker is a free Discord bot that shows Valorant match information for players who link their own Riot account, and a leaderboard for each server. It's provided "as is", without guarantees of availability or accuracy. Match data can be delayed, and Riot's API doesn't provide live data.

Your account

  • Only link a Riot account that you own. Linking requires signing in with Riot.
  • You can unlink at any time with /valorant remove.

Acceptable use

  • Don't use the bot to harass other players.
  • Don't try to overload, abuse or get around the bot's limits.
  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Riot Games' Terms of Service.
